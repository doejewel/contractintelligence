VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2025 ("Effective Date") 
between Acme Corporation Ltd, a company incorporated in Kenya ("Client") and 
TechVendor Solutions Inc., incorporated in Delaware, USA ("Vendor").

1. SERVICES
Vendor agrees to provide enterprise software licensing and support services as described in Schedule A.
The initial term of this Agreement is 24 months from the Effective Date.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
Client agrees to pay Vendor USD $180,000 annually, invoiced quarterly in advance.
Payment is due within 90 days of invoice date. Late payments shall incur interest at 
24% per annum compounded monthly. Vendor may suspend services if payment is overdue by more than 15 days.

3. AUTO-RENEWAL
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 12-month terms unless either party 
provides written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days prior to the end of the then-current term.
Upon each renewal, the fees shall increase by 15% above the previous term's rates without 
further notice or agreement required.

4. LIABILITY
VENDOR'S TOTAL LIABILITY TO CLIENT UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL BE UNLIMITED.
Client acknowledges that Vendor's software is provided for mission-critical operations 
and agrees to indemnify Vendor for any third-party claims arising from Client's use of the software,
including but not limited to regulatory fines, legal fees, and consequential damages.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work product, customizations, integrations, or modifications created by Vendor or 
jointly with Client during the term of this Agreement shall become the exclusive property of Vendor.
Client shall have no ownership rights to any deliverable, including those paid for by Client.

6. DATA PRIVACY
Vendor may collect, process, and share Client's data with third-party analytics providers 
for product improvement purposes. Vendor is not liable for any data breaches caused by 
third-party subprocessors. Client waives any claim under GDPR or applicable data protection laws 
with respect to Vendor's data sharing practices.

7. TERMINATION
Vendor may terminate this Agreement immediately, without cause, upon 7 days written notice.
Client may only terminate for cause after providing Vendor 90 days written notice and 
opportunity to cure. Upon any termination, Client shall pay all fees due through the 
remainder of the current term.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, USA. 
Any disputes shall be resolved exclusively through binding arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware,
with Vendor's counsel serving as the sole arbitrator.

9. NON-COMPETE
During the term and for 5 years after termination, Client agrees not to develop, license, 
or use any software that competes with or replicates the functionality of Vendor's software, 
including any open-source alternatives.

10. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to keep the terms of this Agreement and all shared information confidential 
for a period of 10 years following termination.
